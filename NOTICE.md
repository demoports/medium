# Attribution and provenance

**medium** is an einklang.net production released at The Party 2001. The extracted images, models and music are included for this reconstruction. The original executable and packfile can be downloaded from the release linked below for the optional native verification tools. This port does not grant a new license to those materials.

Original credits identify Pet for code, design and music mix, Chaos for the DirectX engine, Photoniclabor for Makina graphics, and T_jurn for B/W ping-pong. The soundtrack credits shown in the demo are G-Man — “Continue”, Thomas Krome — “The Real” (Adam Beyer mix), and Ultratubes — “Neoplex”. Consult the original production for its complete credits.

The release was obtained from [scene.org](https://files.scene.org/view/parties/2001/theparty01/demo/medium.zip), linked by [Pouët](https://www.pouet.net/prod.php?which=4771).
